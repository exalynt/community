# Exalynt Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Exalynt is committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and respectful community for everyone who participates.

Software engineering benefits from different experiences, perspectives, ideas, and healthy technical disagreement.

We expect participants to treat one another with respect while discussing, building, reviewing, and challenging ideas.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:

- Treating others with respect
- Giving and receiving constructive technical feedback
- Focusing criticism on ideas, designs, and code rather than people
- Asking questions in good faith
- Being willing to explain reasoning and tradeoffs
- Acknowledging that reasonable engineers can reach different conclusions
- Helping others learn
- Taking responsibility for mistakes
- Respecting maintainers' time and project decisions

## Unacceptable Behavior

Behavior that is not acceptable includes:

- Harassment or intimidation
- Personal attacks or insults
- Threats or encouragement of violence
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments
- Deliberate disruption of community conversations
- Repeated bad-faith arguments or trolling
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate sexual content
- Retaliation against someone who raises a legitimate concern

## Technical Disagreement

Exalynt encourages technical discussion and disagreement.

You are welcome to challenge:

- Architectural decisions
- Implementation approaches
- APIs
- Dependencies
- Engineering practices
- Project direction
- Maintainer proposals

Strong opinions are fine. Disrespectful behavior is not.

Critique the idea, explain your reasoning, provide evidence when possible, and give others the opportunity to do the same.

Consensus will not always be possible. Maintainers are responsible for making final decisions about the direction of their projects.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing these standards within Exalynt community spaces.

Depending on the circumstances, responses to unacceptable behavior may include:

1. A private or public request to change the behavior
2. A formal warning
3. Temporary restrictions on participation
4. Removal of comments, issues, discussions, or other content
5. Temporary suspension from the community
6. Permanent removal from Exalynt community spaces

Enforcement decisions should consider the severity, frequency, and context of the behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the Exalynt maintainers.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical.

Information about a report should be shared only with the people necessary to investigate and respond to the situation, except where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect someone's safety.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Exalynt-managed community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Issues
- Pull requests
- GitHub Discussions
- Other official Exalynt communication channels

It also applies when someone is officially representing Exalynt in another community or public space.

## A Community Built Around Engineering

Exalynt exists to inspire and support excellence in software engineering.

That requires an environment where people can ask questions, experiment, disagree, learn, teach, and improve.

Be thoughtful. Be constructive. Respect the people behind the code.
